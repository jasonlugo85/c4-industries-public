WHY C4 EXISTS

Four C's. One family. A business with roots.

C4 is named for Jason's wife, two daughters and dog—all C's. Jason was born in Brooklyn, graduated high school in the Bronx and carried Puerto Rican roots with him through more than two decades of Air Force service.

The brand should look like that story has weight: New York structure, Boricua color, family warmth and the discipline to show the actual product instead of hiding behind generic mockups.
