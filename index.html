Our story

A family business built for the next chapter.

C4 is not a random name. The four C's represent the family behind the business—Jason's wife, their two daughters, and their dog.

Jason left the Bronx at 19 to join the U.S. Air Force. More than two decades later, he is still serving while building what comes next: a small family business grounded in accountability, creativity, and the idea that it is never too late to start something new.

C4 is still early. That is part of the story. We would rather show you what is real, keep improving the work, and build one useful product at a time.

Gaming & Creator Lab

Original ideas for game-loving families and creators.

C4 is researching what younger audiences and creators are actually interested in—from world-building and avatar customization to game-development tools and original meme culture.

Customer care

A good experience continues after checkout.

Digital products should not become a guessing game. Product pages explain what is included, the file format, and how the download is meant to be used.

Are these physical products?

Unless a product page explicitly says otherwise, the items featured here are digital downloads. No physical item is shipped.

Where do I receive my files?

Purchases are completed through the linked storefront. Delivery and download instructions are provided by that storefront after checkout.

Can I see what I am buying first?

Yes. C4 product pages are designed to show actual product pages or artwork whenever possible so you can understand what is included before checkout.

Need help?

Use the support/contact option on the C4 Payhip storefront and include the product name and a brief description of the problem.
