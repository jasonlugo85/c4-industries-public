WHY C4 EXISTS

Built for four C's.

C4 is named for Jason's wife, their two daughters and their dog—all C's. It is a family name before it is a business name.

Jason left the Bronx at 19 to serve in the U.S. Air Force. More than two decades later, he is still serving while building the next chapter: something creative, useful and his family can call their own.

That background shapes the work. Check the details. Be honest about what is ready. Keep improving. Do not pretend activity is the same thing as results.

CUSTOMER CARE

A good experience continues after checkout.

Digital products should not become a guessing game. Product pages explain what is included, the format, and how the download is intended to be used.

Are these physical products?

Unless a product page explicitly says otherwise, the featured items are digital downloads. No physical item is shipped.

Where do I get my files?

Purchases are completed through the linked storefront. Delivery and download instructions are provided after checkout.

Can I see what I am buying?

C4 product pages are designed to show actual pages or artwork whenever possible so you can understand what is included before checkout.

Need help?

Use the support/contact option on the C4 Payhip storefront and include the product name plus a short description of the problem.
