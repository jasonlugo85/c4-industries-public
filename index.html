Jason · Founder, C4 Industries

C4 is named for Jason’s wife, two daughters and dog. All C’s. All family.

Jason was born in Brooklyn, graduated high school in the Bronx and carried his Puerto Rican roots through more than two decades of Air Force service.

That story runs through C4: family memories worth keeping, island roots, borough streets and a love of flight. This is a small, independent studio with those things at its heart.

Before you buy

A few good
things to know.

Choose something you like. Read its listing. Make it part of your home.

What am I buying?

A digital download. Nothing is mailed. Each shop listing explains the included files and delivers them after purchase. Frames and other physical items are not included.

How do I print the mini prints?

The three current mini-print collections include 4 × 6 inch PDFs and US Letter/A4 printing sheets. Open the PDF, choose the sheet for your paper and print at Actual Size / 100%. Check the included print guide before trimming.

Can I give a finished letter as a gift?

Yes. Letters to My Family allows personal household use and finished personal gifts. Blank files may not be resold or redistributed. Check each product’s own license for its terms.

Where can I see the artwork?

Use each product’s shop link to see its current listing and previews. Pictures and videos are temporarily off this website while C4 refreshes its presentation.

How are the products made?

C4 directs, reviews, writes, lays out and packages its downloads with AI assistance. Illustrations are AI-generated with C4 direction and review. Product listings explain the individual scope.

Why are some prices different across shops?

The six featured Payhip downloads are $1.99. Other products and platform pricing can differ; Patreon’s matching downloads are $3. The current price in the shop is the one you pay.
